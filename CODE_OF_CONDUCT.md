# Code of conduct

## The short version

Critique claims, never people. Bring evidence. Assume the other person is arguing
in good faith until they prove otherwise.

## Why this project needs one

quranbench exists so that disagreements about the Quran can be settled by evidence
anyone can re-run, rather than by authority or by volume. That only works if the
space around the evidence stays usable. Religious source material attracts strong
feeling, and a project that invites disagreement has to be clear about the
difference between disagreeing well and disagreeing badly.

## Expected behaviour

- **Argue with the claim, not the claimant.** The site is deliberately built so
  that response pages are keyed to claims and never to people.
- **Show the query.** If you assert something about the text, attach the search
  that produced it. That is the whole point of the tooling.
- **Say when you were wrong.** Investigations have a `revised` and a `withdrawn`
  status precisely so that changing your mind is a normal, visible act rather than
  an embarrassment.
- **Cite accurately.** Misquoting someone's position to make it easier to refute
  wastes everyone's time and is treated as a conduct problem, not a style one.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, harassment, or sustained pursuit of an individual
- Imputing dishonesty, bad faith or disbelief to someone because of a position
  they hold. Say the argument is wrong and show why; do not say the person is a
  liar or an apostate.
- Sectarian abuse, or attacking a person for the tradition they belong to
- Bigotry of any kind, including on the basis of religion, ethnicity, nationality,
  gender, sexuality or disability
- Publishing private information without consent
- Deliberately misrepresenting the corpus, a query, or someone else's investigation

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, in investigations and
responses on the site, and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report privately through the **Security** tab's private reporting, or by opening
an issue saying only that you would like to raise a conduct concern privately.

The maintainer will respond within 7 days. Depending on severity, responses range
from a private word, through editing or removing content, to a temporary or
permanent ban from the repository and from publishing on the site.

Two commitments about enforcement, because a code of conduct that can be used as a
weapon is worse than none:

1. **Holding an unpopular position is not a violation.** This project exists to
   host contested arguments. You will not be sanctioned for a conclusion, only for
   how you treat people while arguing for it.
2. **Enforcement will be explained.** If content is removed or an account is
   restricted, the reason will be stated and it will refer to a specific clause
   above.
