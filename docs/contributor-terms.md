# Contributor terms

**Version 1.0.0 — effective 2026-07-25**

These terms govern everything you contribute to quranbench: investigations,
evidence, responses, annotations, corrections and any other content you create.
You accept them when you create an account, and your acceptance is recorded with
the version and the moment you accepted. You cannot create content without a
recorded acceptance.

They exist for one reason: so that the open dataset can be redistributed freely,
forever, without having to re-contact you. This is decided before your first
contribution because it becomes impossible to fix afterwards (see
`docs/extensibility.md` §2).

## The Quranic text is not yours or ours to license

Nothing here applies to the Quranic text. The source text is immutable, is never
modified, and is always attributed to Tanzil. Contributor terms cover only the
human-created layer built around it.

## What you promise

- The contribution is your own work, or you have the right to license it under

- It does not infringe anyone's copyright, and you did not copy it from a source
  whose licence forbids this.
- You are not contributing machine-translated Quran translations or any text you

## Attribution

Contributors are credited by their **public handle**. Dataset exports carry
per-record attribution to the contributing handle and the contribution's revision
history. Your real name is never published unless you choose to set it as your
display name. Pseudonymous attribution still satisfies CC BY-SA 4.0's attribution
requirement.

## Licence layers, stated separately

- **Corpus data** (tokens, morphology, roots) carries the licences of its
  upstreams — Tanzil (CC BY) and the Leeds Quranic Arabic Corpus (GPL). Those
  obligations propagate and are not changed by these terms.
- **Community data** (your contributions) is CC BY-SA 4.0 as above.
- **Editorial data** (the project's own investigations) may carry its own
  licence, stated on the relevant pages.

## Moderation

Contributions may be removed or hidden under the published moderation policy.
Removal does not revoke the licence already granted on copies in released dataset
versions; it removes the content from the live site.

## Changes to these terms

New versions get a new version number. A change never applies retroactively to
contributions you already made under an earlier version — those remain governed by
the version you accepted at the time.
